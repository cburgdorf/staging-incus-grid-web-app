Questions

How does the free month work? +

Choose the monthly plan and enter your payment details. The first 30 days are free. After that, the normal monthly price is charged unless you cancel before. The free month is available once per account.

Can I cancel anytime? +

Yes. You cancel in the app under Billing. You keep full access until the end of the free month or the period you have paid for.

Yes. You cancel in the app under Billing. You keep full access until the end of the year you have paid for.

What is Lifetime? +

A single payment for permanent full access, including features we add later. There is no subscription. If you already have one, it ends automatically. Lifetime is only offered for a limited time.

What stays free? +

Everything in the Free column, with no time limit and no payment details. You only need a free account, created in a minute.

Which currency do I pay in? +

Prices are shown in euros in Europe and in US dollars elsewhere. Payment is handled by Stripe, which shows the final amount before you pay.

Caution

IncusGrid shows historical patterns for planning. It is not a weather forecast or a navigation aid. Routes are drawn schematically, and boat stays are derived from pauses in boat reports, not confirmed berths. Always check current forecasts, official charts and local conditions before you go to sea.
